# Gamartisan — Online Marketplace for Handmade Art
## User Guide

Gamartisan is a web-based marketplace that connects local **Artists** who handcraft
original artworks with **Buyers** who want to discover, purchase, and support them.
Every purchase also gives back: **10% of every sale** is donated to a partner
charity selected at checkout.

This guide covers how to use Gamartisan as a **Buyer**, as an **Artist (Seller)**,
and as an **Admin**. An installation/getting-started section and a troubleshooting
section are included at the end.

---

## Buyer Guide

### Login
1. Open Gamartisan in your browser.
2. Click **"Login"** at the top of the page.
3. Enter your registered **email** and **password**.
4. Click **"Login"**. You will be redirected to your Buyer Dashboard.

### Create an Account
1. Click **"Join"** (or "Sign Up") at the top of the page.
2. On the **"Join Gamartisan"** page, click **"Register as Buyer"**.
3. Fill in the required fields:
   - First Name
   - Middle Name *(Optional)*
   - Last Name
   - Email Address
   - Password
   - Confirm Password
4. Tick **"I agree to the Terms of Service"**.
5. Click **"Create Buyer Account"**. You are signed in automatically and taken to
   your dashboard.

### Profile & Settings
Go to **"Profile"** from the left-hand sidebar of the Buyer Dashboard.

**Update Profile**
- Edit First Name, Middle Name, Last Name, email, contact number, or address.
- Click **"Save Changes"**.

**Change Password**
- Scroll to the **Security / Change Password** section.
- Enter your **current password**, then the **new password** twice.
- Click **"Update Password"**.

### Browse & Purchase
**Browse Products**
1. Click **"Products"** in the main navigation (or **"Continue Shopping"** from
   the Buyer sidebar).
2. Use the search bar and category filters to narrow down items.
3. Click any product card to open its details page.

**Add to Cart**
1. On the product details page, adjust the **quantity** if needed.
2. Click **"Add to Cart"**.
3. Open the **Cart** icon in the header to review, update quantities, or
   remove items.

**Checkout**
1. From the Cart page click **"Proceed to Checkout"** (or click **"Buy Now"**
   on a product page).
2. Confirm the **quantity** and review the **total price**.
3. Select a **Payment Method**: *Cash on Delivery*, *GCash*, or *Bank Transfer*.
4. Choose a **Charity** from the drop-down — 10% of your purchase is donated
   to this cause.
5. Click **"Place Order"**. You will see an order confirmation and a new
   entry appears under **"My Orders"**.

Out-of-stock items cannot be checked out; you will see an **"Out of Stock"**
notice on the product page.

### My Orders
1. Click **"My Orders"** in the sidebar.
2. View every order with its current status:
   - **Pending** – waiting for the artist to accept.
   - **Processing** – artist is preparing your item.
   - **Shipped** – item is on the way.
   - **Delivered** – item has arrived.
   - **Cancelled** – order was cancelled.
3. Click any order to see item details, artist info, charity, and payment
   information.

### Leave a Review
1. Open any product you have purchased.
2. Scroll to the **"Reviews"** section and click **"Write a Review"**
   *(available only to buyers who have ordered the product)*.
3. Give a **star rating (1–5)** and write your comment.
4. Click **"Submit Review"**. Your review is published immediately and helps
   other buyers.

### AI-Powered Recommendations
On your Buyer Dashboard you will see a **"Recommended For You"** section.
These are products hand-picked for you by Gamartisan's AI based on your past
orders, favourite categories, and similar buyers' activity. Click any
**"AI Pick"** card to view the product.

### Transaction Logs
1. Click **"Transaction Logs"** in the sidebar.
2. View a full history of every payment, donation, and order receipt tied to
   your account.

### Notifications
- Click the **bell icon** in the top-right header to open your notifications
  panel.
- You will be alerted about order status updates, delivery confirmations,
  review responses, and charity receipts.
- Click a single item to open it, or use **"Mark All as Read"** to clear the list.

### Sign Out
Click **"Logout"** at the bottom of the sidebar.

## Artist (Seller) Guide

### Login
1. Open Gamartisan in your browser.
2. Click **"Login"**.
3. Enter your registered **email** and **password**.
4. Click **"Login"**. You will be redirected to your Artist Dashboard.

### Create an Artist Account
1. Click **"Join"** → **"Register as Seller"**.
2. Fill in the required fields:
   - First Name
   - Middle Name *(Optional)*
   - Last Name
   - Email Address
   - About You *(optional artist bio — share your craft story)*
   - Password
   - Confirm Password
3. Tick **"I agree to the Terms of Service and Seller Agreement"**.
4. Click **"Create Artist Account"**.

### Profile & Settings
Click **"Profile"** in the Artist sidebar.

**Update Profile**
- Edit your First Name, Middle Name, Last Name, email, contact number,
  address, and **Artist Bio**.
- Click **"Save Changes"**.

**Change Password**
- Scroll to the **Change Password** section.
- Enter your **current password**, then the **new password** twice.
- Click **"Update Password"**.

### Manage Products
**Add a New Product**
1. Click **"My Products"** → **"+ Add New Product"**.
2. Fill in the product details:
   - Product Name
   - Description
   - Price (₱)
   - Stock / Quantity Available
   - Category
   - Product Image (JPG / PNG / JPEG / GIF, recommended max 2 MB)
3. Click **"Save Product"**. The product is **published** immediately and
   becomes visible to buyers.

**Edit a Product**
1. From **"My Products"**, click the **Edit** icon on any listing.
2. Update fields as needed (name, price, stock, image, description, category).
3. Click **"Update Product"**.

**Delete a Product**
1. From **"My Products"**, click the **Delete** icon on the product.
2. Confirm the deletion. *Note: products tied to existing orders remain in
   order history.*

Stock is automatically reduced as orders come in. When stock reaches 0 the
product is auto-flagged as **Out of Stock**.

### Manage Orders
1. Click **"Orders"** in the sidebar.
2. Review every order placed on your products. Each order shows the buyer,
   product, quantity, and payout (85% of the sale).
3. Update the **Order Status** in order:
   - **Pending → Processing** (you've accepted the order)
   - **Processing → Shipped** (item handed off to the courier)
   - **Shipped → Delivered** (buyer confirms receipt)
   - You may also mark an order as **Cancelled** with a reason.
4. Click **"Save"** to apply the new status. The buyer is notified automatically.

### Transaction Logs
Click **"Transaction Logs"** to see a detailed record of every sale, payout
(85%), platform fee (5%), and charity donation (10%) tied to your products.

### Reviews
Click **"Reviews"** to see all buyer feedback on your products. Reviews cannot
be edited or deleted by the artist; for abusive or fraudulent reviews, contact
the admin (see **Support**).

### AI Sales Insights
Click **"AI Sales Insights"** in the sidebar to see intelligent reports on:
- Best-selling products and categories for your store.
- Sales trend over time (with simple forecasts).
- Top-performing price ranges.
- Recommendations on what to restock or promote next.

This section is auto-generated — simply refresh the page to see the latest
insights.

### Notifications
The **bell icon** in the header alerts you to:
- New orders from buyers.
- Order status changes.
- New reviews on your products.
- Admin announcements.

Click the bell to view; click **"Mark All as Read"** to clear the list.

### Sign Out
Click **"Logout"** at the bottom of the sidebar.

## Admin Guide

### Login
1. Go to the Gamartisan Admin URL (`/admin`) or click **"Login"** from the
   homepage and sign in with an admin account.
2. Enter your **email** and **password**.
3. Click **"Login"**. You will land on the Admin Dashboard.

### Dashboard
On login, admins can view:
- **KPI cards** – Total Revenue, Total Orders, Total Users (buyers + artists),
  Platform Earnings (5%), Total Donations (10%), and Total Products.
- **Order Status chart** – a doughnut chart of Pending, Processing, Shipped,
  Delivered, and Cancelled orders.
- **Recent Orders** – latest orders with buyer, product, amount, and status.
- **Top Selling Products** – ranked list of best-performing products.

### User Management
**Buyers**
1. Click **"Buyers"** in the sidebar.
2. View every registered buyer with their order count and total spent.
3. Open a profile to see order history, transactions, and personal details.
4. Click **"Suspend"** or **"Reactivate"** to toggle the account status.

**Artists**
1. Click **"Artists"** in the sidebar.
2. View every registered artist with product count, sales, and payouts.
3. Open a profile to review their products, orders, and reviews.
4. Click **"Suspend"** or **"Reactivate"** to control marketplace access.

### Product Management
1. Click **"Products"** in the sidebar.
2. Browse every product on the marketplace.
3. Admins can **Create**, **Edit**, or **Delete** any product, as well as
   override **stock** and **availability**.
4. Use filters to find products by category, artist, or status.

### Category Management
1. Click **"Categories"** in the sidebar.
2. Click **"+ New Category"** to add a category (name, description, optional
   image/icon).
3. Click **Edit** or **Delete** on any row to update or remove categories.

### Order Management
1. Click **"Orders"** in the sidebar.
2. Review every order with full details (buyer, artist, product, amount,
   payment method, charity, status).
3. Override the **status** if needed (e.g. mark as cancelled on dispute).
4. Use filters (Pending / Processing / Shipped / Delivered / Cancelled) to
   inspect workflow bottlenecks.

### Charity Management
**Charities**
1. Click **"Charities"** in the sidebar.
2. Click **"+ New Charity"** to register a charity partner (name, description,
   logo, contact, status).
3. **Edit** or **Delete** any charity, or toggle its **Available / Unavailable**
   status so it shows up (or hides) at buyer checkout.

**Donation Summary**
1. Click **"Donation Summary"** in the sidebar.
2. View total donations per charity, per month, with downloadable breakdowns.

### Reports
Click **"Reports"** in the sidebar. Admins can generate and download:
- **Sales Report** – revenue, platform fees, transaction logs by date range.
- **Donations Report** – donations broken down per charity and per date range.
- **Artist Performance Report** – top artists by sales, orders, and ratings.

Each report has a **Download** button that exports the data.


### Review Moderation
1. Click **"Reviews"** in the sidebar.
2. Browse every product review with reviewer, rating, comment, and product.
3. Click **Delete** to remove any review that violates community guidelines
   (spam, harassment, hate speech, false claims, etc.).

### Notifications
The **bell icon** in the header notifies admins about:
- New buyer or artist registrations.
- New orders placed.
- New charity donations recorded.
- Newly submitted reviews.

### Sign Out
Click **"Logout"** at the bottom of the sidebar.

## Installation / Getting Started

### System Requirements
Before using Gamartisan, make sure your device meets the following minimum
requirements.

**Supported Devices**
- Windows laptop / desktop
- macOS laptop / desktop
- Android or iOS mobile devices
- Tablets (Android / iPadOS)

**Supported Browsers** *(always use the latest version for best performance)*
- Google Chrome
- Mozilla Firefox
- Microsoft Edge
- Safari (macOS / iOS)

**Internet Requirements**
- Stable internet connection.
- At least **10 Mbps** recommended for smooth browsing, image uploads, and
  real-time order updates.

**Required Permissions** *(some features need optional permissions)*
- **Camera** – for taking product photos or proof-of-delivery shots.
- **File / Storage Access** – for uploading product images and profile photos.
- **Notifications** *(browser permission)* – for real-time order and review
  alerts.

### Getting Started

**1. Accessing Gamartisan**
1. Open your preferred web browser.
2. Enter the official Gamartisan web address.
3. Press Enter to load the homepage.

**2. Creating a New Account**
1. Click **"Join"** (or **"Sign Up"**).
2. Choose **Buyer** or **Seller** on the Join page.
3. Complete all required fields.
4. Click **"Create Account"** and proceed to your dashboard.

**3. Logging In**
1. Click **"Login"**.
2. Enter your **email** and **password**.
3. Click **"Login"** and you will be routed to the right dashboard based on
   your role (Buyer, Artist, or Admin).

## Troubleshooting

### Password Recovery
1. On the **Login** screen, click **"Forgot Password?"**.
2. Enter your registered **email**.
3. Check your inbox (and spam folder) for the reset link / OTP.
4. Follow the link and set a new password.
5. Log in again with the new password.

### Common Issues & Fixes

| Issue                         | Possible Fix                                                              |
|-------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Page not loading              | Check internet connection; refresh the browser; clear browser cache.       |
| Cannot upload product image   | Ensure the image is JPG/PNG/JPEG/GIF and under ~2 MB; allow storage access.|
| OTP / reset email not received| Check spam folder; wait 1–2 minutes; request a new OTP.                    |
| "Out of Stock" at checkout    | Stock was depleted by another buyer — try another product or smaller qty. |
| Payment method not working    | Switch to an alternative payment method (COD, GCash, or Bank Transfer).    |
| Order still "Pending" for long| Contact the artist through the order page; escalate to admin if unresolved.|
| AI Recommendations are empty  | Place at least one order — AI learns from purchase history.                |
| Cannot suspend a user (admin) | Make sure you are logged in as an admin; refresh and try again.            |
